Problem Statement 2

The Problem

Clearly defined. Clinically honest. Operationally real.

When a pregnant woman reaches a maternal-care consultation, her health history is scattered across paper antenatal cards, handwritten prescriptions, lab reports, WhatsApp photos of records, and notes from previous visits at different facilities. The clinician spends 8–12 minutes of every consult reconstructing what has already happened and what is missing — instead of starting with a clear, verified picture. The woman gets less care time. Gaps go undetected. Conflicts go unresolved.

— Core problem definition · CareLink Handoff · Health-A-Thon 2026

📄

Records are scattered

ANC card at home, prescription at another facility, lab report on a phone, referral note lost in a bag. No one has the full picture.

🏥

Multi-facility care

A woman visits community health worker → PHC → sub-centre → referral hospital. Each visit creates a new paper trail in a new place.

⚡

Conflicting information

Two records show different gestational ages. A drug listed "prescribed" in one, "allergic" in another. Nobody checks — until a crisis.

📵

No connectivity

PHCs, sub-centres, and community health posts often have poor or no internet. Digital solutions that need Wi-Fi simply don't work here.

Existing Market Solutions 4

What Exists Today

Every existing solution either ignores this specific problem or solves it in a way that doesn't work in the real field conditions.

Solution Type Examples What It Does Why It Fails Here
🏥 National EHR Systems ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission), HMIS Central government health records platform. Patients get ABHA ID. Records stored in cloud. Requires internet, smartphone literacy, and active enrolment. Penetration at PHC/sub-centre level is still nascent. Paper cards still dominate.
📱 mHealth Apps Kilkari, NIKSHAY, Sahi Saheli SMS/app reminders for ANC visits, ASHA tracking, nutrition guidance. Reminder tools — not record management tools. Don't capture, verify or organise actual paper records. No consult-ready view.
🤖 AI Diagnostic Tools Niramai, Qure.ai, Wadhwani AI AI reads scans, X-rays, or symptom data to flag clinical risk. Clinical inference tools — high regulatory risk. None designed for administrative record organisation or handoff readiness. Require trained operators.
📋 Telemedicine Platforms MFine, Practo, eSanjeevani Video consultation, e-prescriptions, doctor booking. Assumes connectivity. Doesn't solve the paper record problem. Adds a new digital channel but doesn't bridge existing fragmented history.
📦 Generic EMR/EHR Bahmni, OpenMRS, eVital Open-source Electronic Medical Records systems for clinics and hospitals. Requires data entry from scratch. Doesn't ingest existing paper records. Not designed for multi-facility handoffs. No conflict detection.
💬 WhatsApp for Records No formal product — informal use Health workers photograph and forward records via WhatsApp. Extremely common in India. Unstructured, unverified, no searchable history, HIPAA/data risk. Works — but the information stays scattered in chat threads.
The Gap No One Fills: None of these solutions address the specific problem of organising existing paper records from multiple facilities into a verified, conflict-checked, source-linked, consultation-ready summary — that works offline.

Our Solution 5

What We Build

A physical + digital trust chain — from scattered paper to verified wristband.

📸

Live Camera Extraction

Point a phone at any paper record. AI reads it in real-time — extracting visit dates, facility names, document types. Each field shows a confidence score.

🔗

Source-Linked Facts

Every extracted piece of data links back to the exact source image. Clinician can tap any fact → see the original document. Full traceability.

⚡

Conflict Detection

Automatic rule-based checks compare facts across records. Contradictions are flagged instantly — never silently merged. A human resolves every conflict.

📋

Protocol Gap Detection

Checks patient's records against the government's ANC schedule. If Hb test should have happened by Week 20 and there's no record — flags it as MISSING.

🗣️

Patient Voice Verification

Health worker asks the mother: "When was your last visit?" Her spoken answer is transcribed and matched against document data — a third verification source.

✅

Human-in-the-Loop

Nothing is shown as "verified" until a human confirms it. AI extracts — humans decide. Every fact needs a person before anyone relies on it.

🔌 Hardware 🤖 AI Powered 📴 Works Offline 💡 First of its Kind
💎

NFC Patient Wristband

Patient wears a ₹50 silicone NFC wristband. Any health worker taps their phone → patient journey opens instantly. No app download. No login. Works offline.

🖨️

Thermal Handoff Card

Thermal printer produces a pocket-sized card with key facts + QR code. Works where there's no phone, no internet. Verified data — smaller than a prescription slip.

Design Principles & Safety 7

Why This Wins With Clinician Judges

Every design decision is explained. Every AI boundary is explicit.

✅ What the AI Does

  • ▸Reads records into a fixed administrative schema
  • ▸Extracts visit date, facility name, document type
  • ▸Identifies pending follow-up items mentioned
  • ▸Links every field to its source image
  • ▸Assigns a confidence score to each field
  • ▸Flags low-confidence items for human review

🚫 What the AI Never Does

  • ✗Diagnose any condition
  • ✗Score clinical risk
  • ✗Interpret lab values, BP, or vitals
  • ✗Recommend treatment or medication
  • ✗Act without human confirmation
  • ✗Silently resolve conflicts or missing data

The Three-Source Trust Chain

📄
Document
OCR + Gemini reads the paper record. Confidence scored per field. Source image preserved.
👩‍⚕️
Health Worker
Manually confirms or rejects each extracted fact. Nothing advances to "verified" without this step.
🗣️
Patient Voice
Mother verbally confirms key facts. Spoken answer matched against document data. Raises confidence or flags conflict.
When all three sources agree: Confidence reaches maximum. Status = Verified. The clinician can rely on this fact.
When sources disagree: Flagged as conflict. Never silently merged. A human resolves it before anyone acts.
The answer that wins clinician judges: "CareLink doesn't replace clinical judgment — it ensures the clinician has the complete, verified picture to make that judgment. We organize paperwork. Doctors do medicine."
