Solution Overview, Key Features & Impact

A Closed Physical-to-Digital Trust Chain: Zero-Friction Capture to Verified Triage

System Innovation 05 / 13

1. Multimodal AI Extraction (Gemini 2.5 Flash + PaddleOCR)

Receives raw camera capture and local OCR text stream. Cross-validates handwritten marginalia against printed headers into strict administrative JSON. Confidence heuristic scored (0.1–0.98).

2. Deterministic Conflict Engine (Zero Hallucination)

Rule-based Python engine checks for temporal collisions (visits within 48h at different clinics), gestational week regressions (weeks decreasing chronologically), and duplicate uploads.

3. Indian MoHFW 4-Visit ANC Protocol Auditor

Audits every uploaded document against India's official National Antenatal Protocol. Flags missed blood groups, missing TT-1/TT-2 vaccinations, and skipped ultrasound scans.

4. Source-Anchored Fact Traceability

Every extracted fact maintains a direct foreign-key binding to the original high-resolution image. Clinicians tap any milestone to inspect the original stamped document slip.

Multimodal Prompting

Hard Clinical Boundary

Deterministic Supremacy

Safety-critical conflict checks and MoHFW protocol compliance are coded in immutable Python logic—never entrusted to probabilistic AI outputs.

Safety, Privacy & Responsible AI

The Three-Source Consensus Model & Unbreakable Clinical Boundaries

Clinical Safety First 10 / 13

The Three-Source Consensus

No fact is marked authoritative without multi-source convergence:

  • Source 1 (Document OCR): Extracted from paper with confidence score (0.1–0.98).
  • Source 2 (Health Worker): ANM confirms or corrects extracted fields.
  • Source 3 (Patient Voice): Mother verbally confirms visit dates to catch misfiling.
Consensus = Status: Verified

Zero Clinical Hallucination

Strict boundary engineering guarantees zero diagnostic risk:

  • No Diagnoses: Gemini prompt explicitly forbids predicting clinical outcomes or risk tiers.
  • No Vital Inference: Blood pressure, lab numbers, and heart rates are filtered out.
  • Zero Auto-Merge: Contradictions are never silently resolved by AI; human review is mandatory.
Doctors Practice Medicine · AI Organizes

DPDP 2023 & HIPAA Alignment

Patient privacy and ethical sovereignty built into the architecture:

  • On-Premise Storage: SQLite DB and uploads stay local on clinic machine.
  • Zero Data Retention: Gemini API calls run under enterprise zero-data retention terms.
  • Inclusive Token Access: Non-literate mothers own their data physically via NFC wristbands.
Full Patient Data Sovereignty
Winning Clinician Philosophy: "CareLink doesn't attempt to replace clinical judgment—it ensures the obstetrician has the complete, verified picture to make that judgment under emergency pressure."
