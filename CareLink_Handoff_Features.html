Core Innovation Features 3

Seven Features. Zero Competition.

Each feature is designed so no other team can replicate it during the hackathon.

Hardware First of its Kind Works Offline

① NFC Patient Wristband

A ₹50 silicone NFC wristband the patient wears. Any health worker at any facility taps their phone → the last confirmed patient journey opens instantly. No internet needed. No login. No app download. The patient's verified record travels with them — on their wrist.

Hardware Zero-Tech Fallback

② Thermal-Printed Handoff Card

After confirmation, a 58mm thermal printer prints a pocket-sized card: QR code + key facts in plain text. Works where there's no phone, no internet, no electricity. The patient carries verified, structured data — smaller than a prescription slip. The ultimate offline handoff.

AI Powered Demo Showstopper

③ Live Camera → Real-Time Extraction

Point a phone camera at a paper ANC card. Watch data populate on screen in real-time — visit date, facility, document type, pending items — each with a confidence bar and a source-image link. No slides needed. Just point and watch.

No One Else Will Think of This AI Powered

④ Patient Voice Verification

The health worker asks the mother: "When was your last visit?" She answers: "September 9th at the sub-centre." Her voice is transcribed, matched against the document data, and logged as a third verification source. Document + Worker + Patient = trust chain. When sources agree → confidence rises. When they disagree → flagged for review.

Core Innovation Features (cont.) 4
Smart Logic Clinician-First

⑤ Protocol-Aware Gap Detection

The Indian government's standard ANC protocol defines which visits and tests should happen at which gestational weeks. CareLink checks: "Patient is at 28 weeks, but no Hb test recorded after Week 20" → flags MISSING. This is not clinical advice — it's a checklist against a published government schedule. Like a flight pre-departure check. The clinician instantly sees what's overdue.

Visual Innovation

⑥ Handoff Chain Timeline

The patient's journey rendered as a visual chain — each node is a facility visit, colour-coded by verification status. Green = verified. Yellow = unclear. Red = conflict. Dashed = missing. Clinicians think in journeys, not data tables. One glance shows every gap in the care chain.

Zero Friction

⑦ WhatsApp Forwarding Ingestion

Health workers already share records via WhatsApp. CareLink provides a number — forward any photo of a record to it, and it's auto-ingested into the patient's journey. Zero training. Zero new behaviour. Just forward the image you were going to send anyway.

The Safety Line (repeat this to every judge): CareLink never diagnoses, never scores risk, never interprets lab values, and never acts without human confirmation. It organizes what already exists, verifies it from multiple sources, and puts it in front of the clinician — ready to use.
